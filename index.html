FAQ

Am I eligible?

If you’re aged 13–18, you’re eligible! No prior experience required.

Is this free?

Yes! Hack Club is a nonprofit helping teens build technical projects at no cost.

But I’ve never coded before!

Perfect! Game jams are designed for beginners. You’ll have workshops, mentors, and teammates to help you every step of the way.

What if my parents are concerned?

We’re here to help! You can see our parent guide here, or they can reach out to us at haven@hackclub.com with any questions.

I still have questions!

Join #haven-help on Slack or reach out to us at haven@hackclub.com!

Can I organise a Haven?

Absolutely! We’re always looking for passionate organisers. If you’re ready to bring the magic of game development to your community, we’d love to help.

Why should I organise a Haven?

You’ll make an impact on your community, whether inspiring someone to make their first game or helping someone find friends in tech. Leading an event is usually very difficult, but we’re providing support to help you along the way!

What are the steps to organising?

First, apply through our organiser form. Then we’ll guide you through venue booking, team building, workshop planning, and day-of coordination.

Do we get volunteer hours?

Many schools accept organising hours as community service. If your school requires documentation, we can provide it!

Can I join an organising team?

Of course! Many cities have organising teams. Reach out to organisers in your area or apply to join an existing team.
